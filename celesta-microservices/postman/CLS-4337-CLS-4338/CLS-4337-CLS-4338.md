# CLS-4337-CLS-4338: free-round win for a bonus held in another currency (local)

**Feature:** a `bonus_deposit` naming a Promotion bonus is booked in the bonus's own wallet, whatever currency the session plays in.

## Setup

- Wallet stack up, branch `CLS-4337-CLS-4338` checked out, `bin/console cache:clear`.
- `./run.sh` (one invocation, order matters). `python3 -I gen.py` regenerates the JSON.

## Steps

1. Create EUR and USD wallets for a fresh player, EUR for a second player.
2. Claim free-round bonus A in EUR and B in USD.
3. Credit 5.00 for bonus A from the USD session.
4. Retry the same credit.
5. Bonus information: A = 5, B = 0.
6. Control: credit bonus B from the USD session.
7. Credit an unknown bonus.
8. Second player claims bonus C; first player credits bonus C.

## Variables

| Variable | Example | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `walletUrl` | `http://localhost:88` | wallet nginx |
| `guardKey` | from container | guard HMAC key, `run.sh` reads it |
| `domainId` | `1` | domain |

Captured at runtime: `ownerId`, `otherOwnerId`, `bonusA/B/C`, `bonusUnknown`, `ptx` (random), `eurWalletId`, `txA`.

## Notes

- DEV-ONLY: calls the wallet directly, guard signed. Not PO-runnable.
- Expected: 0 failures on the branch. On master, steps 3, 4, 5 (bonus A), 7 and 9 fail: the credit never lands.
- The amount is not converted: 5.00 from the USD session becomes 5.00 EUR on the bonus.
- The response `balance` is the session wallet's (USD), not the bonus wallet's.
- `initiate-wallets` OpenAPI documents `cashBalance`/`withdrawableBalance`, the form rejects them as extra fields. Pre-existing.
